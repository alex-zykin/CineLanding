# Third-party notices

The CineLanding workflow draws on the continuity and first/last-frame ideas in
[Aironzak/instagram](https://github.com/Aironzak/instagram) at commit
`88e17734e82f86927f132e87bfe337ba1b0116b1`.

`site/app/scroll-sequence.tsx` adapts the upstream scroll-linked frame playback
approach: a tall section, sticky canvas, scroll-to-frame mapping, progressive
loading, and a reduced-motion fallback. The adapted implementation is included
